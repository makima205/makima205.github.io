Who We Are

We are a collective of architects, designers, and thinkers shaping the future of living spaces.

Our Story

Founded in 2010, CreateSpace began with a simple idea: that architecture should not just be about buildings, but about the people who inhabit them.

Over the last decade, we have grown from a small studio in Pune to a distributed team working across India. We believe in "Design with Purpose"—a philosophy that prioritizes sustainability, cultural context, and human well-being above all else.

Whether it is a high-rise in Mumbai or a bamboo retreat in the Northeast, our process remains the same: listen, collaborate, and create.
